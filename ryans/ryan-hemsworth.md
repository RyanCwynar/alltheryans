# Ryan Hemsworth

**Ryan Hemsworth** (born April 23, 1990) is a Canadian DJ and record producer from Halifax, Nova Scotia [1]. His 2013 debut album *Guilt Trips* won the 2014 Juno Award for Electronic Album of the Year [1]. His album *Numbskull* was released on Topshelf Records on October 21, 2026 [2][4].

## Early life and education

Hemsworth was raised in Halifax [1][6] and studied journalism at the University of King's College, according to Wikipedia [1].

## Career

### Guilt Trips and Secret Songs

Wikipedia lists his genres as electronic and hip-hop [1]. In 2011 he released *Distorted*, a collaborative album with Shady Blaze [1]. *Guilt Trips* came out on Last Gang Records in 2013 and won the Juno for Electronic Album of the Year in 2014 [1]. In 2014 he started Secret Songs, a label for music by friends and newer artists. Wikipedia lists Tennyson, GFOTY and Kero Kero Bonito among its releases [1].

Topshelf's biography says he has worked with Alex G, Tinashe and Mitski over a 15-year span [3]. Wikipedia also lists *Alone for the First Time* (2014), *Elsewhere* (2018), the collaborative *Radio Active* (2022, with Bodysync and Giraffage) and *Nutty* (2024) [1].

### Numbskull (2026)

Pitchfork reported on August 12, 2026, that Hemsworth had signed to Topshelf Records. Pitchfork and the label call *Numbskull* his first album, or first solo album, since *Elsewhere* in 2018, although Wikipedia lists *Nutty* (2024) as a studio album [1][3][5]. The lead single was "One Step" [5]. Sarah Tudzin of Illuminati Hotties sings on "One Step" [3][5]. Hemsworth said in a statement that the track started from "old Three 6 Mafia tapes, and some old crunchy 90s UK rave stuff" [5].

The album has 12 tracks and runs 28 minutes [2][4]. Guests include Nana Lourdes, MSPAINT, Tegan and Sara, and Allie [3][5]. The label describes influences from shoegaze, lo-fi and metal, and calls "Never Learned," with Tegan and Sara, an emo track built on guitar and blast beats [3]. Topshelf says CDs and tapes are shipping and vinyl ships in early November 2026 [3]. Pitchfork reported that he would support George Clanton on tour in November 2026 [5].
