# Ryan Cwynar

**Ryan Cwynar** is a software engineer and entrepreneur. He operates **Byldr Software LLC** and is the creator of **All The Ryans** (alltheryans.com), the encyclopedia this page belongs to [1][2][3].

## Byldr
The site byldr.dev presents Byldr as a service that builds and runs self-hosted ecommerce and payment stacks—including automated crypto and stablecoin checkout—for legal merchants that mainstream processors have dropped [1]. It advertises same-day starts, a free first week, and fixed monthly retainers, and states that it works only with legal businesses [1]. The site’s footer and terms state that Byldr Software LLC is operated by Ryan Cwynar and is a Wyoming limited liability company with a Sheridan, Wyoming, business address [1][2].

On byldr.dev, Cwynar lists a résumé of public claims: USAF captain (2008–2016), founder of the development agency Fueled On Bacon (sold; 2014–2021), CTO at PAR1 (2021–2023), and principal engineer at Telcoin working on banking and crypto rails (2023–present) [1]. Those roles are presented here as self-published biography from his commercial site; independent press confirmation was not located for this rewrite [1]. Contact email published on the site is ryan@byldr.dev [1][2].

## All The Ryans
All The Ryans is a public directory of notable people named Ryan. The project’s own documentation describes a Lit frontend, libSQL database, and Hono API, with seed data authored as Markdown under `ryans/` [3]. The live site is published at alltheryans.com [3].
