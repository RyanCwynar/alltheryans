# Ryan Hoover

**Ryan Hoover** is an American entrepreneur and early-stage investor. He founded **Product Hunt**, a community product-discovery site that became a standard launch venue for startups, and later founded **Weekend Fund**, which he continues to run [1][5][6].

## Career

### Product Hunt

Hoover launched Product Hunt in late 2013 as a side project for sharing and discovering new apps and products [2][4]. The site grew into a widely used tech launchpad. In December 2016, **AngelList** acquired Product Hunt; TechCrunch reported the price as about $20 million based on a source close to the deal [1]. Hoover said Product Hunt would remain an independent platform and that he would stay CEO [1][2].

In October 2020, Hoover announced that he was stepping down as Product Hunt CEO and that investor and founder Josh Buckley would take the role. Hoover said he would continue to support the company at a strategic level [5].

### Weekend Fund

In 2017, while still at Product Hunt, Hoover started **Weekend Fund**, initially reported as a $3 million AngelList Angel Fund for early-stage startups [3][4]. As of 2026, Weekend Fund’s site describes writing roughly $250k–$500k checks into early-stage companies worldwide, with a network of founder and operator LPs, and lists a portfolio including companies such as Deel, MoonPay, and Function Health [6].

## Notable work

- Built Product Hunt from an email-list experiment into a category-defining launch community [1][2].
- Sold Product Hunt to AngelList (2016) [1].
- Founded and continues to operate Weekend Fund (2017–present) [3][6].
