# Ryan Graves

**Ryan Graves** (born 1983) is an American businessman and investor. He was the first employee hired at **Uber**, briefly held the CEO title in the company’s early months, later ran global operations, and now leads the investment firm **Saltwater** [1][2][5].

## Career

### Hiring via tweet

In January 2010, Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick tweeted that he was looking for an entrepreneurial product/business-development hire for a pre-launch location-based service. Graves, then in a GE information-technology management training program, replied: “Here’s a tip. email me :)” with his email address [1][3]. He joined Uber on March 1, 2010, as the company’s first employee [1].

### Roles at Uber

Graves served as CEO for roughly a year in 2010, then moved into operating roles as Kalanick took the CEO title; Graves later became senior vice president of global operations for nearly seven years [1][3]. He announced his resignation as an Uber executive in August 2017, shortly after Kalanick’s forced exit as CEO [1][5]. He remained on Uber’s board through the company’s May 2019 IPO period, then resigned from the board effective May 27, 2019, stating it was a personal decision and not a disagreement with the company [2][4]. Forbes has described him as a billionaire from Uber equity [1][5].

### Saltwater

Graves founded **Saltwater** (also referred to as Saltwater Capital), which he has described as focused on majority investments in or acquisitions of small, profitable technology businesses [5][6]. CNBC reported Saltwater portfolio interests including Fort Point Beer, Caliva, and Kindbody; Forbes later summarized the firm as acquiring small, profitable companies [1][5].

## Notable work

- First hired Uber employee (2010), brief early CEO, long-tenured SVP of global operations [1][3].
- Uber board director until May 2019 [2][4].
- Founder and CEO of Saltwater [5][6].

## Public profile

Graves has lived in Hawaii with his family, according to Forbes reporting cited by CNBC [1][5]. He posts on X as @rfrfrfrf.
