# Ryan Castro

**Bryan David Castro Sosa** (born 1994), known professionally as **Ryan Castro**, is a Colombian reggaeton and dancehall singer-songwriter. Nicknamed “El Cantante del Ghetto,” he went from busking on Medellín buses to international hits including “Mujeriego,” “Jordan,” and “Quema” with Peso Pluma [1][2][3].

## Early life

Castro was born in 1994 in the Medellín area of Antioquia and grew up in Pedregal, a northern Medellín barrio [1][3]. *El País* identifies him as Bryan David Castro Sosa [3]. He has said he began singing at bus stops and on buses because he lacked money for studies, using tips to support his household and save for recording [1][2]. Friends from the barrio nicknamed him their “cantante del ghetto” [1]. He later lived with his mother in Curaçao, where Caribbean sounds influenced his music, before returning to Colombia after “Morena” (2017) gained traction [2][6].

## Career

Castro’s 2021 merengue-reggaeton hybrid “Mujeriego” spread widely on TikTok; “Jordan” became a signature hit and charted on Billboard’s Global 200 lists [1][2]. Billboard named him a Latin Artist on the Rise in 2022, noting a Heat Latin Music Award for best new artist and early U.S. touring [1]. He has collaborated with Feid, opened for Karol G in Medellín, and appeared with J Balvin and Maluma [2][3].

In 2023 he sued Kevin Roldán’s King Records in Medellín over management, recording, and publishing disputes [4][5]. In May 2024 an arbitration tribunal ordered the label to pay him more than $2 million and awarded him full ownership of his phonograms and audiovisual masters [4][5].

His debut album *El Cantante del Ghetto* was released May 10, 2024, as an homage to Pedregal, with guests including Arcángel and Jowell & Randy [2][3][4]. “Quema” with Peso Pluma reached the Billboard Hot 100 [2]. Sony Music Latin, his label, lists later projects including *Sendé* and *Hopi Sendé* (2025) among his catalog [6].

## Notable work

- Singles: “Mujeriego,” “Jordan,” “Quema” (with Peso Pluma) [1][2]
- Album: *El Cantante del Ghetto* (2024) [3][4]
