# Ryan Leonard

**Ryan Leonard** (born January 21, 2005) is an American professional ice hockey right winger for the Washington Capitals of the National Hockey League (NHL) [4]. The Capitals chose him eighth overall in the 2023 NHL Draft [1]. He played two seasons at Boston College, where he led the nation in goals as a sophomore and was named Hockey East Player of the Year [1][2]. He captained the United States to the 2025 World Junior Championship gold medal and was named the tournament's most valuable player [1].

Leonard signed with Washington in March 2025 and debuted in the NHL the next day [1][3]. In his first full season, 2025–26, he scored 20 goals and 45 points in 75 games and finished eighth in voting for the Calder Memorial Trophy, the league's rookie of the year award [4].

## Early life and education

Hockey-Reference lists Leonard's birthplace as Northampton, Massachusetts, and his birth date as January 21, 2005 [4]. The Capitals and Boston College both list his hometown as Amherst, Massachusetts [1][2]. He is the son of Cindy and John Leonard and has three siblings, John, Alyssa and Brianna [2]. His older brother John also played college hockey at UMass and went on to play professionally, including in the American Hockey League [2].

At Boston College, Leonard majored in Communication Studies and received two athletic scholarship awards, the George J. Norberg '39 Memorial Scholarship Fund and the Antonacci Family Flynn Fund Scholarship [2].

## Career

### 2021–2023: U.S. National Team Development Program

Before college, Leonard spent two seasons with USA Hockey's National Team Development Program (NTDP) [2]. He scored 51 goals in 2022–23, tied for ninth-most in a single season in program history, and 77 goals across his two seasons, seventh all-time in the program and two behind Auston Matthews [2]. At the 2023 IIHF U18 World Championship he scored 2:20 into overtime of the gold-medal game to beat Sweden 3–2, and finished the tournament with 17 points including eight goals [2]. The Capitals selected him eighth overall in the 2023 NHL Draft [1].

### 2023–2025: Boston College

As a freshman in 2023–24, Leonard played all 41 games and had 60 points on 31 goals and 29 assists [1][2]. His 31 goals were the most by a freshman in Boston College history and ranked third nationally [2]. He was named a second-team All-American and the Most Outstanding Player of the NCAA Providence Regional, and helped Boston College reach the national championship game [1][2].

As a sophomore in 2024–25, he had 49 points on 30 goals and 19 assists in 37 games and led the country in goals and in game-winning goals, with nine [1][2]. He put together a 17-game point streak, the longest in the nation that season [2]. He became the first Division I men's player to score 30 or more goals in back-to-back seasons since Boston College's Cam Atkinson in 2009–10 and 2010–11 [2]. His honors that season included Hockey East Player of the Year, a first-team All-American selection, the Walter Brown Award and a place among the three finalists for the Hobey Baker Award [2]. Over two college seasons he totaled 109 points on 61 goals and 48 assists in 78 games [1].

### International play

Leonard captained the U.S. team at the 2025 IIHF World Junior Championship [1]. He was named the tournament's MVP and best forward after recording 10 points, as the United States won gold for the second year in a row [1]. He also played six games for the senior U.S. team at the 2024 IIHF World Championship, recording one assist [1].

### 2025–present: Washington Capitals

The Capitals signed Leonard to a three-year, entry-level contract with an average annual value of $950,000 on March 31, 2025 [1]. He made his NHL debut on April 1, 2025, in a 4–3 win at Boston [3][6]. In his third NHL game, on April 4, 2025, he scored his first NHL goal into an empty net at 18:24 of the third period, sealing a 5–3 win over the Chicago Blackhawks [5]. That night Alex Ovechkin scored twice to tie Wayne Gretzky's NHL career record of 894 goals [5]. "It's cool to score an empty-netter, but at the end of the day I witnessed history tonight," Leonard said [5]. He finished the 2024–25 regular season with one goal in nine games and had one assist in eight playoff games [4].

In 2025–26, his first full NHL season, Leonard played 75 games and recorded 20 goals, 25 assists and 45 points [4]. CBS Sports and RotoWire reported that he also had 153 shots and 125 hits that season [7]. He finished eighth in Calder Trophy voting and also received All-Rookie Team votes, according to Hockey-Reference [4]. The Capitals missed the playoffs that season [8].

Washington opened the 2026–27 season on October 2, 2026, with a 5–2 win at Carolina [7]. Leonard scored in the game while playing on the top line with Dylan Strome and Ovechkin [7]. The team's season preview noted that this trio was the only forward line returning intact from the previous season [8]. "Obviously, we're a pretty deep team," Leonard said after the game [10]. Through nine games, the Capitals' public relations account reported that he had eight points [9]. Hockey-Reference lists him at four goals and four assists in nine games for the season through October 20, 2026 [4].

## Notable work and achievements

- Scored the overtime winner in the 2023 U18 World Championship gold-medal game [2].
- Set Boston College's record for goals by a freshman, with 31 in 2023–24 [2].
- Led NCAA Division I in goals (30) and game-winning goals (9) in 2024–25, and won Hockey East Player of the Year [1][2].
- Captained the United States to the 2025 World Junior gold medal and was named tournament MVP [1].
- Scored his first NHL goal on the night Ovechkin tied Gretzky's goals record [5].
- Recorded 45 points as an NHL rookie in 2025–26 and placed eighth in Calder Trophy voting [4].
