# Ryan Fleury

**Ryan Fleury** is an American software engineer who works on development tools in the games industry and writes at **Digital Grove** (dgtlgrove.com) [1][6][7]. He is a Senior Engine Programmer at Epic Games associated with RAD Game Tools and is the lead developer of the open-source **RAD Debugger** [6][7].

## Career

### Epic Games and the RAD Debugger

Fleury’s public GitHub profile lists his company as Epic Games (RAD Game Tools) and his location as Washington, USA [6]. His SPLASH 2026 conference profile states that he is a Senior Engine Programmer at Epic Games and the lead developer of the RAD Debugger, that he has worked on the debugger since 2021, and that his focus areas include process control, debug-information formats, high-performance UI, and the debugger’s evaluation compiler and visualization architecture [7].

In his Better Software Conference 2025 talk, he said he works on the RAD Debugger, that the project was originally started by RAD Game Tools, and that RAD Game Tools is now part of Epic Games; he also stated he was speaking on his own behalf rather than as a company representative [4]. On Digital Grove he likewise described the talk as covering his RAD Debugger work on evaluation and visualization systems [3].

Epic Games announced on January 7, 2021 that it had acquired the technology and business of RAD Game Tools [8][9]. The RAD Debugger repository is published under the Epic Games GitHub organization; its README describes a native, user-mode, multi-process graphical debugger that (as documented there) supports local Windows x64 debugging with PDBs, along with related RAD Debug Info (RDI) and RAD Linker work [5].

### Digital Grove

Fleury publishes **Digital Grove**, a personal Substack project at dgtlgrove.com (also reachable via rfleury.com) [1][11]. The About page identifies him as a professional programmer who works in the gaming industry on development tools and states that Digital Grove is not affiliated with his employer [1]. In June 2023 he described Digital Grove as personal work on alternatives in computing—media, community, and a codebase—while remaining an employed full-time programmer [2]. The site’s index lists essay series on debuggers and UI programming, general programming essays, and podcast appearances [10].

## Public talks and appearances

In July 2025 Fleury spoke at the Better Software Conference on realtime debugger visualization architecture; he posted about the talk on Digital Grove and the conference published a recording on YouTube [3][4]. His SPLASH 2026 profile lists a talk titled “A Compiler And Visualizer Design For Debugger Visualization Expressions” and links his X account as https://x.com/rfleury and GitHub as ryanfleury [7]. He appeared as a guest on the Handmade Dev Show at Handmade Seattle 2022 [12].

## Education

His SPLASH 2026 profile states that he studied undergraduate computer science at the University of Colorado and that he started programming by making games and custom engines [7]. Those details are from his conference profile and were not independently confirmed in other sources checked for this page [7].
