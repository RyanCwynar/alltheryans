# Ryan Humphries

**Ryan Humphries** is an American sales leader and entrepreneur based in Austin, Texas [1][2]. Public information about him is sparse and comes mainly from his own LinkedIn profile and posts rather than independent press [1][2][3].

## Career
According to his LinkedIn profile and a July 2025 post, Humphries joined Curv Group on July 13, 2023, and has worked in sales leadership connected to Curv’s custom-product brands, including Floatie Kings [1][2]. In that post he wrote that he began leading a team of eight while still consulting for other clients, later went full time, and that the team had grown to 24 people after two years [2]. His LinkedIn summary describes helping brands with custom products and lists Curv-related properties such as Floatie Kings, Statik Co, and KeySmart [1].

LinkedIn also lists him as founder of **Zen Growth**, described there as a sales consultancy for agencies and high-ticket teams, with a company page stating a 2023 founding year and an Austin headquarters [1][3]. The same profile lists him as a co-founder of **reserv**, a ticketing venture he described as on hold [1].

His LinkedIn experience history also references earlier ventures including Digital Thrust and Fueled on Bacon as prior roles, again without independent corroboration in the sources reviewed for this page [1][2].
