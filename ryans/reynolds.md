# Ryan Reynolds

**Ryan Rodney Reynolds** (born October 23, 1976) is a Canadian-American actor, producer, and entrepreneur. He is best known for starring as the Marvel antihero Deadpool, and for building consumer brands and a Welsh football club alongside film work [1][2][3].

## Early life and education

Reynolds was born in Vancouver, British Columbia, the youngest of four brothers in an Irish Catholic family [1][2]. He began acting as a teenager after an open casting call for Nickelodeon’s Canadian teen soap *Hillside* (aired in the U.S. as *Fifteen*, 1991–93) [1][2]. After graduating from Kitsilano Secondary School in 1994, he briefly attended Kwantlen College, then moved to Los Angeles in his late teens to pursue acting [1][2].

## Career

Reynolds’s early Hollywood breakthrough was the ABC sitcom *Two Guys, a Girl and a Pizza Place* (1998–2001), followed by the college comedy *National Lampoon’s Van Wilder* (2002) [1][2]. He worked across romantic comedy (*The Proposal*, 2009), action (*X-Men Origins: Wolverine*, 2009; *Green Lantern*, 2011), and contained drama (*Buried*, 2010) [1][2].

The R-rated *Deadpool* (2016), shot in Vancouver, became a major commercial hit and earned Reynolds a Golden Globe nomination; he returned for *Deadpool 2* (2018), receiving a writing credit [1][2]. In 2024 he starred in *Deadpool & Wolverine*, which *The Hollywood Reporter* reported had taken in more than $1 billion worldwide and benefited from an unusually large brand-partnership campaign run with Disney and Reynolds’s company Maximum Effort [5][7].

Beyond Deadpool, Reynolds has appeared in films including *Free Guy*, *The Adam Project*, and *Pokémon Detective Pikachu*, and has produced through Maximum Effort, founded in 2018 [1][7].

## Business and sports

In 2018 Reynolds acquired a stake in Aviation American Gin and became its creative face; Diageo later bought the brand’s parent in a deal reported at up to $610 million, with Reynolds remaining creatively involved [1][2][6]. In 2019 he became a part owner and spokesperson for Mint Mobile; after T-Mobile closed its acquisition of Mint’s parent in May 2024, Reynolds continued in a creative role for the brand [1][4].

With actor Rob McElhenney, he completed a takeover of Welsh club **Wrexham AFC** on February 9, 2021, investing an immediate £2 million after a November 2020 fans’ vote [3]. Their ownership has been documented in the reality series *Welcome to Wrexham* [1].

## Personal life

Reynolds was married to Scarlett Johansson from 2008 to 2011 and married Blake Lively in 2012; they have children together [1][2]. He became a naturalized U.S. citizen in 2018 and was made an Officer of the Order of Canada in 2024 [1].
