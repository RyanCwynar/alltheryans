# Ryan Florence

**Ryan Florence** is an American web developer and educator who created the first version of React Router in May 2014 [1]. With Michael Jackson, he co-founded React Training in 2015 [2][3]. He later co-founded Remix Software with Michael Jackson, with Kent C. Dodds also described as a co-founder in interviews [2][9].

Shopify acquired Remix on October 31, 2022, and said the Remix team would join Shopify while Remix remained an independent open-source framework [4][5]. In October 2026, Florence's public posts described a live view of self-learning bots he was running in Final Fantasy XI [7][8].

## Early life and education

No birth date, birthplace or early-life details were found in accessible reputable sources, so those fields are left blank rather than inferred [6]. A LinkedIn summary surfaced in search results lists economics study at BYU-Idaho from 2001 to 2005, but the profile was not opened in full, so no degree is asserted in the frontmatter [6]. Florence said in a 2024 interview that he had been doing web work for about two decades [10].

## Career

### 2011–2015: Instructure and React Router

Search results for Florence's LinkedIn profile list work at Instructure from 2011 to 2015 [6]. In his own history of Reach Router, Florence wrote: "In May of 2014 I created the first version of React Router and was involved with the project all the way to the end of 2017 at version 4" [1]. He wrote that Reach Router was influenced by React Router and by ideas from its contributors [1].

### 2015–2020: React Training, Reach Router and Reach UI

Florence and Michael Jackson opened React Training in 2015 [2]. Remix's 2021 announcement said they had "helped thousands of teams all over the world be successful with React" through React Training [2]. The same post credited their open-source work on React Router, Reach Router and Reach UI, and said that when someone ran `npm install react-dom`, they also installed React Router or Reach Router about seven times out of ten [2]. React Training's blog described Florence returning to the company in 2019 after a year working apart, and listed continued work on React Router, Reach Router and Reach UI [3].

### 2020–2022: Remix

Florence and Jackson worked on Remix for about 18 months before its October 2021 seed announcement [2]. Remix announced $3 million in seed funding led by OSS Capital on October 11, 2021, and said Remix would be released under the MIT license [2]. The company described Remix as a framework built on web standards, including HTTP methods and caching, the fetch API and progressive enhancement [2].

### 2022–present: Shopify

On October 31, 2022, Shopify announced that Remix and its team were joining Shopify [4]. TechCrunch reported the same day that Shopify had acquired Remix [5]. Shopify said Remix would continue as an independent open-source framework and that Shopify would use it across projects including Hydrogen [4]. Shopify's post named Florence and Jackson as Remix's founders and said they created React Router in 2014, which it said had been downloaded from npm almost a billion times at that time [4].

### How Remix was described by its makers and by Shopify

Remix's seed announcement said the framework starts from web fundamentals rather than static-site generation, using HTTP status codes and caching, HTML prefetching and the fetch API [2]. Shopify's acquisition post said Remix links, buttons and forms work before JavaScript loads, which it presented as progressive enhancement [4]. Shopify also said Remix was edge-first and could run at the edge, in service workers and in Node [4]. Shopify said it would use Remix across several projects, including Hydrogen, its React framework for custom storefronts [4].

Florence's Reach Router history said he wanted "a more accessible web, especially in React," and that focus management was a particular goal [1]. That accessibility aim is stated in his own words in the search-extracted text, and is attributed to him here [1].

## Notable work and achievements

- Created the first version of React Router in May 2014 and worked on it through version 4 in 2017 [1].
- Co-founded React Training with Michael Jackson in 2015 [2][3].
- Co-created Reach Router and Reach UI, credited alongside React Router in Remix's announcement [2].
- Co-founded Remix, which raised a $3 million seed round in 2021 and was acquired by Shopify in 2022 [2][4][5].

## Public profile and social media

Florence posts on X as @ryanflorence [7]. His X bio in October 2026 described him as "giving agency to self-learning Final Fantasy XI bots atm" [7]. On October 1, 2026, a showcase summary of his post described him using Claude Code to build a headless Final Fantasy XI bot client in which decisions were made by an agent system, with a goal of killing mobs efficiently for an experience-point chain [8]. On October 18, 2026, he posted a live view of his Final Fantasy XI bots and wrote that he could show people what he was doing lately with that view [7].
