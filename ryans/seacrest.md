# Ryan Seacrest

**Ryan John Seacrest** (born December 24, 1974) is an American television and radio host and producer. As of 2025–2026 he hosts *American Idol* and *Wheel of Fortune*; he previously co-hosted *Live with Kelly and Ryan* from 2017 until April 2023 [1][2][4][5][6].

## Early life and education

Seacrest was born in Atlanta, Georgia, and grew up in the Dunwoody area [1][2]. He interned and then worked on air at a hometown radio station while in high school, left the University of Georgia before graduating, and moved to Los Angeles to work in entertainment [1][2].

## Career

### Radio and early television

Seacrest hosted early TV including ESPN’s *Radical Outdoor Challenge* and later built syndicated radio franchises: *On Air with Ryan Seacrest* and, from 2004, *American Top 40* after Casey Kasem [1][2]. He has also been a longtime host of ABC’s *Dick Clark’s New Year’s Rockin’ Eve*, becoming the lead host after Clark’s death in 2012 [2].

### American Idol

In 2002 he co-hosted Fox’s *American Idol* with Brian Dunkleman and became sole host from season two; the show made him a national figure [1][2]. After Fox ended the series in 2016, he returned when ABC revived it in 2018 [1][2]. In September 2025 *The Hollywood Reporter* reported he re-signed to host the 2026 season (ninth on ABC, 24th overall), remaining the only on-camera personality present for the show’s entire run [6].

### Live with Kelly and Ryan

Seacrest joined Kelly Ripa as permanent co-host of ABC’s daytime *Live* in May 2017; the show was retitled *Live with Kelly and Ryan* [1][2][3]. In February 2023 AP reported he would leave that spring, with Ripa’s husband Mark Consuelos set to replace him and the show becoming *Live with Kelly and Mark* [3]. His final episode as co-host aired April 14, 2023; Consuelos took over April 17 [4]. He does **not** currently host *Live*.

### Wheel of Fortune

Sony Pictures Television announced in 2023 that Seacrest would succeed Pat Sajak on *Wheel of Fortune* beginning in 2024 [1][2]. He debuted as host on September 9, 2024, also serving as a consulting producer [5].

### Production

In 2006 he formed Ryan Seacrest Productions, whose credits include *Keeping Up with the Kardashians* and related reality series [1][2].
