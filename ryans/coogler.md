# Ryan Coogler

**Ryan Kyle Coogler** (born May 23, 1986) is an American filmmaker. He directed *Fruitvale Station* (2013), *Creed* (2015), *Black Panther* (2018), *Black Panther: Wakanda Forever* (2022), and the original horror film *Sinners* (2025), and co-founded the production company Proximity Media [1][2].

## Early life and education

Coogler was born in Oakland, California, the eldest of three sons of Joselyn Coogler, a community organizer, and Ira Coogler, a juvenile-probation officer; the family later lived in Richmond [1]. He attended St. Mary’s College of California on a football scholarship as a chemistry major, then transferred to California State University, Sacramento, after St. Mary’s dropped football, graduating with a business degree in 2007 [1]. He earned an MFA in film and television production from the USC School of Cinematic Arts in 2011, where he made award-winning short films including *Locks*, *Fig*, and *Gap* [1].

## Career

### Fruitvale Station and Creed

Coogler’s first feature, *Fruitvale Station* (2013), dramatizes the final day of Oscar Grant, an Oakland man fatally shot by a transit officer in 2009. Produced with Forest Whitaker’s company and starring Michael B. Jordan and Octavia Spencer, it won the Sundance Grand Jury Prize and Audience Award [1][2]. He next co-wrote and directed *Creed* (2015), a *Rocky* spin-off centered on Adonis Creed (Jordan) mentored by Sylvester Stallone’s Rocky Balboa; the film was a critical and commercial success and earned Stallone a Supporting Actor Oscar nomination [1][2].

### Black Panther

Marvel Studios hired Coogler to write and direct *Black Panther* (2018), the first MCU film with a Black lead and a predominantly Black cast, and the first MCU film directed by an African American [1]. The film grossed about $1.35 billion worldwide, received seven Oscar nominations including Best Picture, and won three Oscars [1]. He returned for *Black Panther: Wakanda Forever* (2022), made after Chadwick Boseman’s death; it grossed about $859 million and received five Oscar nominations [1].

In 2018 Coogler founded **Proximity Media** with his wife, Zinzi Coogler, and producer Sev Ohanian. The company produced *Judas and the Black Messiah* (2021), among other titles [1].

### Sinners (2025)

*Sinners* (2025), which Coogler wrote, directed, and produced, is a Jim Crow–era Mississippi Delta horror film starring Michael B. Jordan as twin brothers who open a juke joint and confront vampires [1][2]. Britannica reports it grossed about $370 million globally, received a record 16 Academy Award nominations in 2026, and won four Oscars including Best Original Screenplay for Coogler; he was also nominated for Best Director and Best Picture (as producer) [1]. *The New Yorker* profiled the film as his first fully original feature story after four prior features [2].
