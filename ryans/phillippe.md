# Ryan Phillippe

**Matthew Ryan Phillippe** (born September 10, 1974) is an American actor. He rose from daytime television to late-1990s teen films and later dramatic roles in *Crash* and Clint Eastwood’s *Flags of Our Fathers* [1][5].

## Early life

Phillippe was born in New Castle, Delaware, to Richard Phillippe, a chemical technician, and Susan Phillippe, who ran a home day-care center [1][5]. His surname is pronounced “FIL-ip-ee” [1].

## Career

At 17 he joined ABC’s soap *One Life to Live* (1992–93) as Billy Douglas, widely described as daytime’s first openly gay teenage character; Phillippe later told *People* (via Variety) that he was afraid to take the part but saw immediate impact in viewer mail [1][2]. Feature work followed with *Crimson Tide* (1995), *White Squall* (1996), and the teen horror hit *I Know What You Did Last Summer* (1997) [1].

In 1998–99 he starred in *54*, *Playing by Heart*, and *Cruel Intentions*, a modern *Les Liaisons dangereuses* opposite Reese Witherspoon and Sarah Michelle Gellar that sealed his teen-heartthrob period [1][4]. He then moved into ensemble and adult drama: Robert Altman’s *Gosford Park* (2001), for which the cast won a Screen Actors Guild award; Paul Haggis’s *Crash* (2004), another ensemble SAG winner; Eastwood’s *Flags of Our Fathers* (2006) as Navy corpsman John Bradley; and *Breach* (2007) opposite Chris Cooper [1]. Later film and TV credits include *Stop-Loss*, *MacGruber*, *The Lincoln Lawyer*, *Damages*, *Shooter*, and *Big Sky* [1][5].

## Personal life

Phillippe married Reese Witherspoon on June 5, 1999; they have a daughter, Ava (born 1999), and a son, Deacon (born 2003). They announced their separation in October 2006 [1][4].
