# Ryan Smith

**Ryan Smith** (born June 28, 1978) is an American businessman, the co-founder and executive chairman of experience-management software company **Qualtrics**, and the owner of the NBA’s **Utah Jazz** and NHL’s **Utah Mammoth** through Smith Entertainment Group [1][7][8][9].

## Early life and Qualtrics

Smith co-founded Qualtrics in Provo, Utah, in 2002 with his brother Jared Smith, building on technology developed with their father, Scott Smith, a BYU researcher [2][7]. The company grew from a basement startup into a major enterprise software business focused on customer, employee, product, and brand experience data [7].

In November 2018, days before a planned IPO, SAP agreed to acquire Qualtrics for $8 billion in cash—then among the largest private enterprise software deals [4][5]. The acquisition closed in January 2019; Smith continued to lead Qualtrics within SAP [5]. Qualtrics later spun out and went public again in January 2021, with Smith serving as chairman after handing the CEO role to Zig Serafin [6][7]. Forbes notes Silver Lake later took Qualtrics private again in 2023 [9]. Qualtrics lists Smith as founder and executive chairman [7].

## Sports ownership

In late 2020, Smith and his wife Ashley agreed to buy the Utah Jazz and related assets from the Miller family; the NBA Board of Governors approved the sale in December 2020 [1][2][3]. Smith became NBA governor of the franchise with final authority over business and basketball operations [3]. Deseret News and team materials have emphasized his longtime Jazz fandom and prior jersey-patch sponsorship via the cancer charity 5 For The Fight [1][3].

In April 2024, Smith Entertainment Group obtained an NHL franchise in Utah by purchasing the Arizona Coyotes’ hockey assets. The club played 2024–25 as the **Utah Hockey Club**, then adopted the permanent name **Utah Mammoth** on May 7, 2025, after a fan-voting process [8].

## Notable work

- Co-founded Qualtrics (2002); led it through SAP’s $8 billion cash acquisition (announced 2018, closed 2019) [4][5][7].
- Executive chairman of Qualtrics [7].
- Majority owner, Utah Jazz (2020–present) [3].
- Owner, Utah Mammoth (franchise 2024; name 2025) [8].
