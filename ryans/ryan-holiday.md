# Ryan Holiday

**Ryan Holiday** (born June 16, 1987) is an American author and marketer [6]. His own biography says he dropped out of college at 19 to apprentice under Robert Greene, author of *The 48 Laws of Power* [1]. He later worked as director of marketing at American Apparel and founded the creative agency Brass Check [1].

Holiday is known for books applying Stoicism to modern life, including *The Obstacle Is the Way* (2014), *Ego Is the Enemy* (2016) and *The Daily Stoic* (2016) [1][2][3]. Penguin Random House describes him as one of the world's bestselling living philosophers and says his books appear in more than forty languages and have sold over 10 million copies [3]. That publisher total is more recent than the "more than 2 million copies in thirty languages" figure on his own older About page [1].

On October 16, 2026, he posted a video titled "5 Books That Are Better Than an MBA" on X [8]. As retrieved via the fxtwitter API, that post had about 1,403 likes and 1,642 bookmarks [8].

## Early life and education

Search summaries report that Holiday was born in Sacramento County and grew up in the Sacramento area, including Granite Bay [6]. Those summaries say he attended the University of California, Riverside, studying political science and creative writing, and dropped out at 19 [6]. Holiday's own About page confirms the age-19 dropout and the apprenticeship under Greene, without naming the university [1]. Because the birth and schooling details were not opened in a primary source for this draft, they are flagged in the frontmatter basis and should be confirmed against a primary interview before publication [6].

Holiday has described the Greene apprenticeship as starting as a job transcribing interviews between Greene and 50 Cent, then expanding as he did the work well [4]. That account is from an interview summary surfaced in search, so the detail is attributed to his telling rather than stated as independently verified [4].

## Career

### 2006–2012: Greene, American Apparel and Brass Check

Holiday says he dropped out at 19 to apprentice under Robert Greene [1]. He says he had a marketing career at American Apparel and then founded Brass Check, which he says advised clients including Google, TASER and Complex, and authors including Neil Strauss, Tony Robbins and Tim Ferriss [1]. Those client claims are his own biography's claims and are attributed as such [1].

### 2012–2013: First books

Holiday's first book, *Trust Me, I'm Lying: Confessions of a Media Manipulator*, was published in July 2012 [5]. Business Insider covered the book in 2013 in connection with his marketing work [5]. His next book, *Growth Hacker Marketing*, was first published in September 2013, according to search summaries of his bibliography [6]. The marketing claims in *Trust Me, I'm Lying* are his own account of his methods, so later coverage should be attributed to him where it repeats them [5].

### 2014–2016: The Stoicism books

*The Obstacle Is the Way* was published in May 2014 by the Penguin Group, according to search summaries [6]. Holiday's book page says it draws on Stoicism and on Marcus Aurelius's line, "The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way" [2]. He published *Ego Is the Enemy* in June 2016 and *The Daily Stoic*, co-authored with Stephen Hanselman, in October 2016 [6]. His own site lists *The Daily Stoic* among his books and describes it as 366 meditations drawn from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus and other Stoic figures [2].

### 2016–present: Daily Stoic, later books and the bookstore

Search summaries report that Holiday founded The Daily Stoic in 2016, with Hanselman as co-author of the book [7]. Penguin Random House lists later books including *Stillness Is the Key* and a series on the Stoic virtues, and repeats that he lives outside Austin [3]. His own About page lists ten books at the time it was written, including *Conspiracy* and *Stillness Is the Key* [1]. Because his bibliography has grown since that page, no total book count is asserted as current in the body [1][3].

Texas Monthly's coverage, surfaced in search, reports that Holiday and his wife Samantha opened The Painted Porch Bookshop in Bastrop, Texas, in 2021 [7]. Penguin Random House confirms that his bookstore, The Painted Porch, sits on Main Street in Bastrop, Texas [3]. The bookstore is in Bastrop, not Austin; his home is described as outside Austin [3].

### The Stoic books as described by their publisher pages

Holiday's book page describes *The Obstacle Is the Way* as drawing on Stoicism, defined there as enduring adversity with perseverance and focusing on what can be controlled [2]. It presents historical figures including John D. Rockefeller, Amelia Earhart, Ulysses S. Grant and Steve Jobs as examples used in the book [2]. Those are descriptions of the book's contents from his own book page, not independent assessments of its influence [2].

His page describes *The Daily Stoic* as offering 366 days of Stoic insights, with translations from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca and Epictetus, and lesser-known figures including Zeno, Cleanthes and Musonius Rufus [2]. Penguin Random House lists a *Daily Stoic* boxed set and a 10th anniversary edition among his books [3]. Penguin Random House also lists a series on the Stoic virtues among his work [3].

## Notable work and achievements

- *Trust Me, I'm Lying* (2012), his first book, about his own marketing methods [1][5].
- *The Obstacle Is the Way* (2014), which his book page says became a cult classic among athletes, coaches and other public figures [2]. That reception claim is the publisher/author page's claim, attributed here to that page [2].
- *Ego Is the Enemy* (2016) and *The Daily Stoic* (2016, with Stephen Hanselman) [6].
- Founder of Brass Check, according to his own biography [1].
- Co-founder/owner of The Painted Porch in Bastrop, Texas [3][7].
