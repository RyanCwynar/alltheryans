# Ryan Cohen

**Ryan Cohen** is a Canadian-born entrepreneur and investor best known as co-founder of online pet retailer **Chewy** and as chairman and CEO of **GameStop** [1][5][6].

## Career

### Chewy

Cohen co-founded Chewy in 2011 with Michael Day, building an e-commerce pet-supply business known for high-touch customer service [2][4]. In April 2017, PetSmart agreed to acquire Chewy for **$3.35 billion**, then widely reported as the largest e-commerce acquisition [1][4]. The deal closed later in 2017; Cohen continued as Chewy CEO and joined PetSmart’s board [2]. In March 2018, PetSmart announced he was stepping down from day-to-day CEO duties [3].

### GameStop and investing

Through **RC Ventures**, Cohen built a large stake in GameStop and became a central public figure in the January 2021 “meme stock” short-squeeze episode. He joined GameStop’s board in 2021 and later became executive chairman; in September 2023 the company named him CEO (initially described as unpaid) [5].

As of 2026, GameStop’s investor relations materials still identify Cohen as chairman and CEO. In 2026 the company said he asked the board to withdraw a proposed CEO performance award so leadership could focus on operating performance and a proposed acquisition of eBay [6].

## Notable work

- Co-founded Chewy (2011); sold to PetSmart for $3.35 billion (2017) [1][2].
- Activist investor via RC Ventures; GameStop chairman and, from 2023, CEO [5][6].

## Public profile

Cohen’s sparse, often cryptic posts on X (@ryancohen) are closely watched by retail traders. His public reputation mixes Chewy’s operational success with GameStop’s meme-stock volatility [5].
