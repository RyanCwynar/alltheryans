# Ryan Craig

**Ryan Craig** (born January 6, 1982) is a Canadian ice hockey coach and former centre who is head coach of the Vegas Golden Knights of the NHL [1][3]. He was named the franchise's fifth head coach on June 17, 2026, replacing John Tortorella [1][2]. He was an assistant on the Vegas staff that won the Stanley Cup in 2023 [1].

## Early life

Craig was born in Abbotsford, British Columbia [3][5]. He played parts of six seasons, from 1997 to 2003, with the Brandon Wheat Kings of the WHL, two of them as captain, while Kelly McCrimmon was the team's general manager [2].

## Playing career

Tampa Bay drafted Craig in the eighth round, 255th overall, of the 2002 NHL Entry Draft [3][4]. The Lightning say he was a taxi-squad player during the team's 2004 Stanley Cup run, before his NHL debut [4]. He did not play in that postseason; his first NHL game came in 2005–06, when he scored in his debut and finished his rookie season with 15 goals and 28 points [4].

He played 184 games for Tampa Bay from 2005 to 2009 and spent seven seasons in the Lightning organization, then signed with Pittsburgh as a free agent on July 3, 2010 [4][5]. Craig has described the rest of his career as two seasons with the Pittsburgh organization and five with Columbus [4]. He signed a two-way contract with the Blue Jackets in July 2013 [5]. His NHL totals were 198 games, 32 goals and 31 assists [3]. In the AHL he captained Wilkes-Barre/Scranton, Springfield and Lake Erie, and Lake Erie won the Calder Cup in 2016 [4][5]. He retired in 2017 after 14 professional seasons [4].

## Coaching career

Craig joined the Golden Knights as an assistant coach for the franchise's first season, 2017–18, and stayed six seasons [1][2]. Vegas reached the playoffs in five of those six seasons and won the Stanley Cup in 2023 [1].

In June 2023 he became head coach of the AHL Henderson Silver Knights [1][5]. Henderson's win totals rose each season under him, from 28 to 29 to 39. The 2025–26 team went 39-21-12 with 90 points, both franchise records, and lost in the second round of the Calder Cup playoffs to the Colorado Eagles [1].

On June 17, 2026, McCrimmon named Craig head coach of Vegas [1]. Tortorella had replaced Bruce Cassidy on March 29, 2026, and Vegas lost the Stanley Cup Final to the Carolina Hurricanes in Game 6 [6]. Craig had not been an NHL head coach before. At his introduction he said, "I believe I'm ready for this" [6]. Wikipedia reports that Vegas beat the Chicago Blackhawks 5–2 in his first game as head coach, the 2026–27 opener [5].

## Personal life

Wikipedia reports that Craig is married to Jaydee, from Souris, Manitoba, and that they have three children [5]. Craig told the Lightning that the two oldest were born in Tampa [4].

## Recognition

- Stanley Cup, 2023, as Vegas assistant coach [1].
- Calder Cup, 2016, as Lake Erie Monsters player [4][5].
