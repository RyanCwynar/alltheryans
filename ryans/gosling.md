# Ryan Gosling

**Ryan Thomas Gosling** (born November 12, 1980) is a Canadian actor and musician. He moved from child television to acclaimed independent films and major studio hits, earning Academy Award nominations for *Half Nelson*, *La La Land*, and *Barbie* [1][2][3].

## Early life and education

Gosling was born in London, Ontario, and raised largely in Cornwall in a working-class Mormon household [1][2][3]. He has described difficulty in school linked to ADHD and was homeschooled for a period starting around age ten [1]. At 12 he won a place on *The All-New Mickey Mouse Club* (1993–95), performing alongside future stars including Britney Spears, Christina Aguilera, and Justin Timberlake [1][2]. He later appeared on Canadian series such as *Are You Afraid of the Dark?* and *Goosebumps*, then in *Breaker High* and as the lead of *Young Hercules* [1][2].

## Career

Gosling’s early film work included *Remember the Titans* (2000) and a breakout lead in *The Believer* (2001), which won the Sundance Grand Jury Prize [1][2]. *The Notebook* (2004), opposite Rachel McAdams, made him a mainstream romantic lead [1][2]. He followed with darker roles, notably the Independent Spirit Award–winning turn in *Half Nelson* (2006), which brought his first Oscar nomination for Best Actor [1][2].

A 2011 slate—*Drive*, *Crazy, Stupid, Love.*, and *The Ides of March*—cemented his range across thriller, comedy, and political drama [1][2]. He wrote and directed *Lost River* (2014), which was poorly received, then rebounded with *The Big Short* (2015) and Damien Chazelle’s *La La Land* (2016), winning a Golden Globe and a second Best Actor Oscar nomination [1][2]. Later credits include *Blade Runner 2049* (2017), *First Man* (2018), *The Gray Man* (2022), Greta Gerwig’s *Barbie* (2023)—which earned him a Best Supporting Actor Oscar nomination—and *The Fall Guy* (2024) [2]. Britannica notes he starred in *Project Hail Mary* (2026), adapted from Andy Weir’s novel [2].

As a musician he co-founded Dead Man’s Bones with Zach Shields; their self-titled album and 2009 tour featured children’s choirs [1].

## Personal life

Gosling has two daughters with partner Eva Mendes, whom he met while making *The Place Beyond the Pines* (2012); the couple keeps a low public profile [1][2].
