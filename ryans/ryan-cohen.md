# Ryan Cohen

**Ryan Cohen** (born 1986, according to Wikipedia) is a Canadian businessman and investor. He co-founded the online pet retailer Chewy in 2011 and is chairman and chief executive of GameStop [1]. PetSmart bought Chewy in 2017 for $3.35 billion, which Fortune and Wikipedia describe as the largest e-commerce acquisition at the time [1][3].

## Early life

Cohen was born in Montreal to a Jewish family. His mother was a teacher and his father, Ted Cohen, ran a glassware importing business [1][4]. He did not attend college, and he has named his father as his main influence [1][3]. According to Forbes, he started his first business at 15, collecting referral fees from e-commerce sites [4].

## Career

### Chewy (2011–2018)

Cohen founded the company as MrChewy in 2011, at 25 [1]. Cohen has said he pitched more than 100 venture capital firms and was turned down by all of them [1][3]. Wikipedia records a first outside investment of $15 million from Volition Capital in 2013 [1].

PetSmart acquired Chewy in April 2017 for $3.35 billion [1][3]. Cohen stayed on as CEO, and Forbes says he grew annual revenue to $3.5 billion before stepping down in 2018 [4]. Chewy went public in June 2019 [1].

### RC Ventures and Bed Bath & Beyond

Cohen founded the investment firm RC Ventures in 2019 [3]. Fortune reports that RC Ventures held just under 10% of Bed Bath & Beyond in 2022, then sold the stake later that year for a $68 million gain [3].

### GameStop (2021–present)

Forbes reports that Cohen spent about $76 million on a 13% stake in GameStop in late 2020 [4]. He joined the board in January 2021 with two former Chewy executives and became chairman on June 9, 2021 [1][4]. He took over as CEO on September 28, 2023 [1]. Wikipedia reports that he receives no salary, cash bonus or time-vesting stock from GameStop [1]. Fortune reports that the board has promised him stock options worth up to $35 billion if the company's market value reaches about $100 billion [3].

As CEO he cut costs, closed stores and moved the company toward collectibles [1][3]. Fortune reports that full-time salaried staff fell from 11,000 in 2023 to 6,500 in 2025 and that the company held about $9 billion in cash [3]. In May 2026, Fortune reported that GameStop had made an unsolicited offer of about $56 billion for eBay [3].

## Personal life

Forbes lists Cohen as married, a Canadian citizen, and a resident of Bal Harbour, Florida [4].
