# Ryan Carniato

**Ryan Carniato** is a software engineer and the creator of SolidJS, an open-source JavaScript library for building user interfaces [1][2]. The project's GitHub repository was created in April 2018 and describes Solid as "a declarative, efficient, and flexible JavaScript library for building user interfaces" [1]. Since September 2024 he has worked at Sentry as a Staff engineer on its open-source team, a role that funds his full-time work on Solid [3][4].

## Education

His DEV Community profile lists a B.A.Sc. in computer engineering from the University of British Columbia [3]. No birth date or birthplace was found in the sources opened for this page.

## Career

### SolidJS

Solid's contributors page lists Carniato as project founder on the core team [2]. Solid is built on fine-grained reactivity, in which state is held in reactive primitives called signals rather than re-running whole components. Carniato describes himself as a "Fine-Grained Reactivity super fan" and also lists himself as a core team member of the Marko framework [3]. As of November 2, 2026, the solidjs/solid repository had about 36,200 stars on GitHub [1].

In his 2024 Sentry announcement he wrote that "Solid and Signals has completely transformed the way modern JavaScript frameworks are designed over a few short years," and that the next phase would revisit Solid's reactive system for the first time in six years on the way to Solid 2.0, including work on async signals [4].

### Netlify (about 2022–2024)

Before Sentry, Carniato spent about two years at Netlify, which he wrote "believed in us (and me) before anyone else did" and whose support "made SolidStart possible," Solid's application framework [4]. He worked with Netlify's Frameworks and Primitives team and gave talks alongside its developer experience team [4].

### Sentry (2024–present)

On September 3, 2024, Carniato announced he was joining Sentry, the error-monitoring and tracing company, which would support his continued full-time work on SolidJS and open source [4]. His profiles list his role as Staff engineer, open source [2][3].
