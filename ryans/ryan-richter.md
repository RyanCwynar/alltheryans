# Ryan Richter

**Ryan Richter** (born April 12, 1989) is an American soccer coach and former professional player who is the interim head coach of the Philadelphia Union of Major League Soccer [1][2]. He was named to the role on May 27, 2026, when the Union were last in MLS at 1-10-4 [2][3]. Under Richter the team went unbeaten in 15 straight league matches (12 wins, 3 draws) after the 2026 FIFA World Cup break and clinched a 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs berth on October 28, 2026 [4][5].

## Early life and education

Richter played at William Tennent High School in Warminster, Pennsylvania, and then at La Salle University in Philadelphia, where he was named Atlantic 10 Offensive Player of the Year in 2010 and scored 28 goals with 13 assists [1]. The *Inquirer* describes him as a native of Southampton, Bucks County [3].

## Playing career

The Union drafted Richter fifth overall in the 2011 MLS Supplemental Draft and signed him on March 1, 2011; he scored his first goal for the club that August before his option was declined in January 2012 [1]. He then played for the Charleston Battery, winning the 2012 USL Pro championship, Toronto FC, Ottawa Fury FC, where he won the 2015 NASL Fall Championship, Bethlehem Steel FC, as its first captain, and the New York Cosmos [1]. He retired after the 2017 season [1].

## Coaching career

Richter joined the Union's academy as a coach in 2018 and was a first-team assistant from 2022 to 2024 [1][2]. He was appointed head coach of Philadelphia Union II in MLS Next Pro in January 2025 [1][2].

On May 27, 2026, the Union parted ways with head coach Bradley Carnell, named Jon Scheer sporting director and made Richter interim head coach while starting a "global search" for a permanent coach [2][3]. Carnell had been named MLS Coach of the Year after winning the 2025 Supporters' Shield, but the 2026 team entered the World Cup break with one win in 15 matches [3]. Richter's first match in charge was set for July 22 against Red Bull New York [3].

After the break the Union went 15 league matches without a loss, which MLSsoccer.com recorded as 12 wins and 3 draws [4]. They clinched a playoff place on October 28, 2026, during a 0-0 draw at Supporters' Shield winners Nashville SC, after New England beat Red Bull New York [5][6]. It was the club's 10th postseason appearance [5]. With two regular-season matches left the Union were sixth in the Eastern Conference at 13-11-8, which works out to 12-1-4 in league play since Richter took over [3][4].
