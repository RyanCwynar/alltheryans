# Ryan Serhant

**Ryan Matthew Serhant** (born 1984) is an American real estate broker, entrepreneur and television personality [16][2]. He founded and leads **SERHANT.**, a New York–based residential brokerage that also runs media, education and technology divisions [12][16]. He first became widely known as a cast member of Bravo's *Million Dollar Listing New York*, which premiered in 2012 [1][11]. Since June 2024 he has been the central figure of the Netflix reality series *Owning Manhattan*, which follows his company [15][16].

Serhant studied English literature and theater at Hamilton College and worked as an actor in New York before he entered real estate in 2008 at Nest Seekers International [1][3][6]. There he built the Serhant Team, which HousingWire reported was New York City's No. 1 team by sales volume in 2017 [6]. He launched his own firm in September 2020 [12][13]. He has written sales and business books, including *Sell It Like Serhant* (2018) and *Big Money Energy* (2022) [7][8].

## Early life and education
Serhant was born in Houston, Texas, and grew up outside Boston [2][6]. He told the *Boston Herald* that he grew up on a farm in Topsfield, Massachusetts, on the North Shore, in a "very, very strict household." He said his father had him work "for like a buck an hour, raking the gravel" so that he would "understand the value of a dollar" [1]. He credited that upbringing for "any and all success I have now" [1].

He was active in theater at the Pingree School in South Hamilton, Massachusetts [1]. He then graduated from Hamilton College in New York with a degree in English literature and theater [1][4].

## Career

### 2006–2008: Acting
After college, Serhant moved to New York City to work as an actor [6]. He played Evan Walsh on the CBS soap opera *As the World Turns* in 2007 and 2008 [1]. HousingWire reported that the character was "killed off shortly after" his arrival [6]. HuffPost reported that he also performed on Broadway a few times and modeled [4]. HousingWire described a brief stint as a hand model, after which he "turned to real estate to pay the bills" [6].

### 2008–2020: Nest Seekers International
Serhant joined Nest Seekers International in 2008 [3]. He told HousingWire that he made $9,000 in his first year, and that he set a goal of meeting five new people every day [6]. The *Boston Herald* reported in 2012 that he had sold $102 million in property in his first two years as an agent, and that he had become a senior vice president and managing director at the firm [1]. In 2012 he told *The Real Deal* that Nest Seekers founder Eddie Shapiro was the reason for his success: "I would not be where I am today if it weren't for him, 1,000 percent" [3].

His group at the firm, the Serhant Team, grew quickly. HousingWire reported in September 2018 that the 62-person team had closed $333.1 million in Manhattan sales in 2017, earning the title of New York City's No. 1 broker [6]. *The Real Deal* ranked the team No. 2 among residential brokers, with $480.5 million in closed sales in 2018 [13]. SERHANT.'s launch announcement said the team had completed more than $4 billion in sales since 2008, including more than $1.4 billion in closed and in-contract sales in 2019 [12].

### 2012–2021: *Million Dollar Listing New York*
Shapiro put Serhant forward to audition after the show's production company contacted Nest Seekers [3]. *Million Dollar Listing New York*, a Bravo series following high-end Manhattan agents, premiered on March 7, 2012 [1]. Serhant called filming "the best time of my life," because "I got to do real estate, which I love, and it brought back the cameras" [1]. *People* described the series as "Emmy-nominated" and said it launched the television careers of Serhant and Fredrik Eklund [11]. HuffPost reported in 2017, during the sixth season, that Serhant said the show was not staged and that Bravo checked clients and listing agreements [4]. Serhant also starred in a spin-off, *Sell It Like Serhant* [6].

His acting work continued on a small scale. He played a hedge-fund manager in Noah Baumbach's *While We're Young* (2015), opposite Ben Stiller and Naomi Watts. He joked to the *Houston Chronicle* that his father said, "Good thing I didn't blink" [2][4].

In July 2022, *People* reported that Bravo had paused *Million Dollar Listing New York* and that the ninth season, which premiered in May 2021, would be Serhant's last [11]. A production source told the magazine that he had decided this when he started his own company [11].

### 2020–present: SERHANT.
On September 15, 2020, Serhant announced SERHANT., which he described as "a multidimensional real estate brokerage that sits at the intersection of media, entertainment, education and technology" [12]. The firm combined his brokerage, an in-house film studio, and his online sales course. The company said the course had more than 4,000 agents in over 100 countries [12]. Serhant told Bravo's website that he believed "the traditional real-estate brokerage model is broken" [9].

At first he kept a dual license with Nest Seekers to finish his existing listings and developments [14]. *The Real Deal* reported that some members of his team at Nest Seekers had not been told about the timeline. His team had fallen from 62 agents to 40, while the new firm had 20 employees [13]. Inman reported that the new company would put short-form video on its YouTube channel at the center of its marketing [14].

The company's site lists divisions including SERHANT. Studios (film-quality content), ID Lab (branding and marketing), New Dev (new construction sales), ADX (listing exposure technology) and S.MPLE (sales technology) [16]. In 2022, *People* reported that Serhant was expanding SERHANT. into South Florida [11].

### 2024–present: *Owning Manhattan*
*Owning Manhattan*, a Netflix reality series about SERHANT., premiered in June 2024. Its second season premiered on December 5, 2025, according to the company [16]. In a December 2025 *Variety* interview, Serhant called the first season "a startup story" and said the show brought the company "a million followers and subscribers." He also said the firm had raised a large seed round for property technology and was opening in 12 more states [15]. *Variety* noted that the second season shows Serhant having an emotional breakdown on camera. He told the magazine, "No one cries for the person at the top" [15]. The series' executive producers, Fenton Bailey and Randy Barbato of World of Wonder, had also produced *Million Dollar Listing* [15].

## Notable work and achievements
- Led the Serhant Team at Nest Seekers, which closed $333.1 million in Manhattan sales in 2017 [6]. The company says the team completed more than $4 billion in sales between 2008 and 2020 [12].
- Founded SERHANT. (2020) [12][13].
- Cast member of *Million Dollar Listing New York* (2012–2021) and star of *Sell It Like Serhant* [1][6][11].
- Central figure of Netflix's *Owning Manhattan* (2024–) [15][16].
- Books: *Sell It Like Serhant*, a sales guide whose UK publisher describes it as a playbook to "sell just about anything" (published in the UK on September 20, 2018) [7]; *Big Money Energy* (Hachette, on sale February 1, 2022), in which the publisher says he writes that ten years earlier he was "living paycheck-to-paycheck and didn't even own a suit" [8].

## Public profile and social media
Serhant has built his business around social media. In 2020, Bravo reported that he had 1.5 million Instagram followers and more than 1 million YouTube subscribers [9]. He told *The Wall Street Journal*, as quoted by Bravo, that "it's an attention game. It's not who has the better postcards, it's about who can attract the most eyeballs" [9]. He has described his motto as "Expansion, always in all the ways" [15].

Coverage of the SERHANT. launch also noted friction. *The Real Deal* reported tension with Nest Seekers, and reported that members of his old team said they had not been told what would happen to them [13]. Serhant told the publication, "This wasn't a sit-down with my entire team" [13].

In interviews he has often described sales in plain, practical terms. In 2015 he told the *Houston Chronicle* that home staging was the best investment a seller could make, comparing a listing to "going on a first date" [2]. In 2017 he told HuffPost that agents "fight for listings all the time" and "pitch against each other all the time" [4].

## Personal life
In July 2016, Serhant married Emilia Bechrakis, a lawyer, in the Pontikonisi church on the Greek island of Corfu. *People* reported that 150 friends and family attended [5]. On February 26, 2019, after what Serhant described as years of trying, including IVF, the couple had a daughter [10].

## Recognition
- *Million Dollar Listing New York* was a two-time Emmy nominee during his tenure, according to SERHANT.'s 2020 announcement [12].
- HousingWire reported that his team was New York City's No. 1 team by sales volume in 2017 [6].
