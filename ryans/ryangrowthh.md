## Public profile and social media
On October 21, 2026, he posted that Grok Bot "helped me generate ~2M views in my first 2 weeks on the platform" [2]. The post listed 10 ways he used it [2]:

1. An X researcher bot that scans outlier content overnight and delivers a morning report of content ideas for his niche.
2. A "Chief of Staff" bot that manages his MCP connections and routines.
3. An X article tracker for logging article ideas by text instead of in Notion.
4. An analytics coach that sends a daily report of his impressions and replies.
5. A competitor watch that tracks his X lists.
6. A "Bookmarks Brain" that logs his bookmarks, sends a daily summary and summarizes long-form content.
7. A quote post scout that finds high-performing posts in his niche to quote.
8. A fact checker for stats and claims in other people's posts.
9. A "sparring" bot for testing X ideas.
10. A banner and visual prompter that writes image prompts for article banners and post graphics.

In the same post he suggested a bot to find paid promotions and sponsors [2]. As of October 21, 2026, his profile showed about 4,685 followers [1], and the post had about 58,000 impressions and 1,550 bookmarks [2].
