# Ryan Anderson

**Ryan Anderson** is an American entrepreneur and former litigator who co-founded **Filevine**, a Salt Lake City–area legal technology company, and serves as its CEO [1][2].

## Career

Anderson has said he built Filevine with co-founders Jim Blake and Nathan Morris because existing legal tools did not capture lawyers’ real communication-heavy workflow [1]. TechCrunch reported the company was founded in 2014 and, by April 2022, had raised a $108 million Series D led by StepStone Group (total funding then about $155 million), with roughly 2,900 organizational customers and 42,000 users [1].

In a 2024 Utah Business piece, Anderson wrote that Filevine had progressed from a credit-card–funded start to a company “officially valued at over a billion dollars by outside investors” [5]—i.e., unicorn status by that self-reported investor mark.

In September 2025, Filevine announced **$400 million** in equity financing closed across two rounds over about 15 months, with Insight Partners, Accel, and Ryan Smith’s Halo Fund among leaders [2][3][4]. Law.com and Upstarts reported the financing took the company to an estimated **$3 billion** valuation [3][4]. Anderson described Filevine as embedding AI into a “legal operating intelligence system” rather than forcing lawyers to use separate AI tools [2].

## Notable work

- Co-founded Filevine (2014) as a legal case/practice management SaaS company [1].
- Raised a $108M Series D (2022) [1].
- Raised a combined $400M (2024–2025) at an estimated $3B valuation [2][3][4].

## Public profile

Anderson posts on X as @ryandersonlaw. Coverage often situates Filevine within Utah’s “Silicon Slopes” tech community and notes Halo Fund / Ryan Smith’s later investment role [4].
