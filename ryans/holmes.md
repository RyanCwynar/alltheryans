# Ryan Holmes

**Ryan Holmes** is a Canadian entrepreneur best known as the founder of **Hootsuite**, the Vancouver-based social media management platform. As of 2026 he again leads the company as CEO after returning on an interim basis [1][5][6].

## Career

### Invoke and Hootsuite’s origins

Holmes ran digital agency **Invoke**, where clients needed a way to manage multiple social accounts. He and Invoke colleagues built an internal tool that launched publicly on November 28, 2008 (initially under another name before a naming contest produced “Hootsuite”) [1][2]. The free dashboard product grew quickly; Hootsuite’s own history says it reached 100,000 users by the following summer and later scaled into a global enterprise product [1][2].

### Growth and funding

Hootsuite spun into a standalone company and raised large venture rounds. In August 2013 it announced a $165 million Series B led by Insight Venture Partners with Accel and OMERS Ventures—among the largest Canadian software financings of its era—bringing total funding to nearly $187 million, according to TechCrunch [3].

### CEO transitions

Holmes stepped down as CEO in 2020 when Hootsuite named former Zendesk COO Tom Keiser CEO; Holmes remained board chair [4]. In April 2026, GeekWire reported that Holmes returned as interim CEO, succeeding Irina Novoselsky [5]. Hootsuite’s leadership page lists him as CEO and founder [6].

## Notable work

- Founded Hootsuite (2008) out of Invoke [1][2].
- Led the company through major growth financing, including the 2013 Series B [3].
- Returned as interim CEO in 2026 [5][6].

## Public profile

Holmes is a prominent Canadian tech founder and posts on X as @invoker. Hootsuite remains headquartered in Vancouver with customers worldwide [2].
